Privacy Policy

The short version. Halmoni stores the care information you enter so your family can share it. We do not sell it, we do not advertise against it, and we do not use it to train anything. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

This policy explains what Halmoni collects, why, where it goes, and what control you have over it. Halmoni is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY] ("we", "us"). It applies to the Halmoni mobile app and to halmoni.uk.

1. What we collect

Your account

Your email address, used to sign you in. Halmoni signs you in with a one-time code sent to your email — we never ask for, store, or see a password.

Your caregiver profile

Your display name, and optionally your relationship to the person being cared for, a phone number, a colour, and a photo.

Information about the person you care for

This is the heart of the app, and it is health information. Depending on what you choose to enter, it can include:

  • Their name, nickname, photo and date of birth
  • Medical conditions, allergies, blood type, and care preferences
  • Emergency contacts, healthcare proxy and resuscitation preferences
  • Their doctor, pharmacy and insurance details
  • Medications — name, dose, purpose, schedule, prescriber, refill dates
  • Every dose logged: when it was due, when it was given, by whom, or skipped
  • Appointments and notes taken at visits
  • Symptoms, their severity, and when they were observed
  • Care hand-offs between family members, and who is currently on duty
  • Messages and notes your family writes to each other in the app

Everything above is entered by you or your family. Halmoni does not import records from any pharmacy, insurer, or health provider, and has no connection to any medical record system.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or files beyond images you attach yourself.
  • No biometrics. If you turn on Face ID or Touch ID, that check happens entirely on your device through Apple's system. Your face or fingerprint is never sent to us and we never receive it.

2. Entering information about another person

Halmoni is built for recording someone else's health information. You are responsible for having the authority or that person's agreement to record and share it with the family members you invite. If they ask you to remove it, please do — you can delete any record, or the whole family, from inside the app.

3. Who can see it

The family members you invite. That is the point of the app: an invited member can see the care records for that family, including doses, medications and notes.

Access is enforced in the database itself, per row, not merely hidden in the app — someone outside your family cannot read your family's records even if they query our API directly. Invite codes expire after 14 days and can be revoked.

Our staff do not read your care data as a matter of course. We may need to access records to investigate a fault or a safety issue you report to us.

5. On your device

So the app works without a signal, Halmoni keeps a copy of your family's records on your phone and syncs when you reconnect. That copy is protected by your device's own encryption and passcode, and by the app lock if you enable it. It is removed when you delete your account or sign out.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account:

  • If other members remain in your family, your account and membership are removed. The records your family has already logged stay with them, because they are the family's shared history and not yours alone.
  • If you were the last member, the entire family is deleted — every parent profile, medication, dose, appointment, symptom and note.
  • The copy on your device is wiped either way.

Backups may retain data briefly after deletion before they age out.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your information. Most of this you can do directly in the app. For anything else, or to object to how we handle your data, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is enforced per row in the database. Sign-in uses one-time email codes, so there is no password to steal. You can add a Face ID / Touch ID lock to the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

9. Children

Halmoni is for adults coordinating care. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
