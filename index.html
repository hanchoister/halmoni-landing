Questions

We’d ask too.

Is my family's data private? +
Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only invited family members see your care plan. We never sell data. Row-level security means one family's data never touches another's.
Is Halmoni free? +
Free during private testing. Launch pricing hasn't been set. Waitlist members get early-access pricing when we open the App Store.
Does my parent need to use it? +
No. Halmoni is for whoever's doing the caring — you, or you and your siblings. Your parent doesn't need an account or an app.
What if my sibling won't join? +
Then it's just for you, which is common. Halmoni still puts everything in one place — and the day someone does step in, you can hand them the whole picture in one message.
