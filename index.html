Frequently asked

Questions we hear a lot.

Is Halmoni free? +

Yes — during private beta, Halmoni is free for every family on the waitlist. When we launch publicly, plans start at a flat monthly rate per family (not per sibling). Early-access families get founding pricing for life.

Who can see my parent's data? +

Only the family members you invite. No advertisers. No data brokers. We don't sell, share, or train models on your data — full stop.

Does my mom need to install anything? +

No. Halmoni is for the adult children coordinating care — your parent doesn't have to use it, install it, or even know it exists (though many do, and love it).

How is this different from a shared note or spreadsheet? +

Notes and sheets are passive — you still have to remember to check them. Halmoni is active: refill countdowns, missed-dose alerts, side-effect pattern detection, and hand-offs mean the app tells you what needs attention, not the other way around.

When is Halmoni launching? +

Currently in private beta with a small group of families. Public launch on the App Store is planned for later this year — join the waitlist to be first in line.
