Features

Everything caregiving siblings need — nothing they don't.

💊

Medications, without the chaos

Track every med, every dose, every refill. Your siblings see what you gave and when — no more "did anyone remember?" texts at 9pm.

🩺

Doctor visits that don't get lost

Visit Mode captures diagnoses, new prescriptions, and follow-ups as they happen. Everyone in the family sees the summary that night.

🔄

Hand off the mental load

Going out of town? Hand off to a sibling with a message. They accept — and now they're on-duty. You can actually take a break.

✨

Spot side effects before they escalate

When new symptoms show up after a med change, Halmoni flags it. So you can bring it to the doctor — not discover it in the ER.

📓

One timeline, one truth

Every dose, symptom, note, and visit lives in a single feed. Scroll it before a doctor's appointment and walk in prepared.

🌿

Private by design

Your family's data belongs to your family. No ads. No data resale. Your parent isn't a product.
