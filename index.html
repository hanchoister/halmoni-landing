Questions

We’d ask too.

Is my family's data private? +
Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only invited family members see your care plan. We never sell data. Row-level security means one family's data never touches another's.
Is Halmoni free? +
Free during private beta. Launch pricing hasn't been set. Waitlist members get early-access pricing when we open the App Store.
Does my parent need to use it? +
No. Halmoni is designed for the caregivers — the siblings coordinating care. Your parent doesn't need an account or an app.
What if my sibling won't join? +
Even solo, Halmoni replaces spreadsheets, sticky notes, and mental overhead. Most families onboard one sibling at a time — and it snowballs.
