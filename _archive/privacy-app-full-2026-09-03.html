Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

Before publishing: replace privacy@halmoni.uk, Hana Choi and [ADDRESS] throughout this page, and confirm the transfer safeguard named in section 6. This banner must be deleted before the page goes live.

The short version. Halmoni stores the care information you enter so your family can share it. We do not sell it, advertise against it, or use it to train models. It is held on servers in the United States. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

This policy explains what Halmoni collects, why we are allowed to hold it, where it goes, and what control you and the person you care for have over it. It covers the Halmoni mobile app and halmoni.uk.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Hana Choi of [ADDRESS] is the data controller for the information described here. That means we decide what is collected and why, and we are the ones you hold accountable for it. Contact: privacy@halmoni.uk.

2. What we collect

Your account

Your email address, used to sign you in. Halmoni sends a one-time code to your email — we never ask for, store, or see a password.

Your caregiver profile

Your display name, and optionally your relationship to the person being cared for, a phone number, a colour, and a photo.

Health information about the person you care for

This is the heart of the app, and in data protection law it is special category data — the most strongly protected kind. Depending on what you enter, it can include:

  • Their name, nickname, photo and date of birth
  • Medical conditions, allergies, blood type, and care preferences
  • Emergency contacts, healthcare proxy, and resuscitation preferences
  • Their doctor, pharmacy and insurance details
  • Medications — name, dose, purpose, schedule, prescriber, refill dates
  • Every dose logged: when due, when given, by whom, or skipped
  • Appointments and notes taken at visits
  • Symptoms, their severity, and when they were observed
  • Care hand-offs between family members, and who is on duty
  • Messages and notes your family writes to each other in the app

All of it is entered by you or your family. Halmoni does not import records from any pharmacy, insurer or health provider, and has no connection to any medical record system.

Website visitors

If you join the waiting list on halmoni.uk we keep the email address you give us, and use it only to tell you when Halmoni launches. You can ask us to remove it at any time. Our host records standard server logs, including IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos or files beyond images you attach.
  • No biometrics. If you turn on Face ID or Touch ID, that check happens entirely on your device through Apple’s system. Your face or fingerprint is never sent to us and we never receive it.

3. Why we are allowed to hold it

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time by deleting the information or your account. Withdrawing does not affect what we did lawfully beforehand.

4. The person you care for

Halmoni is built for recording someone else’s health information, so that person has rights over it even though the account is yours.

If you are the person being cared for and want to see what is held about you, correct it, or have it deleted, write to privacy@halmoni.uk. You do not need a Halmoni account and you do not need to go through your family. We will verify your identity and respond within one month.

If you are the caregiver: you are responsible for having that person’s agreement, or the legal authority to act for them, before entering their information and sharing it with the family members you invite. If they ask you to remove it, please do.

5. Who can see it

The family members you invite. That is the point of the app: an invited member can see that family’s care records.

Access is enforced in the database itself, row by row, not merely hidden in the app — someone outside your family cannot read your family’s records even if they query our API directly. Invite codes expire after 14 days and can be revoked.

Our staff do not read your care data as a matter of routine. We may need to access records to investigate a fault or safety issue you report to us.

6. Where it is stored, and who processes it

Your information is stored in the United States (Northern Virginia), because that is where our database is hosted. If you are in the UK or the EEA, this is an international transfer. We rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with our host to protect it, and you can ask us for a copy of those terms.

7. Automated insights

Halmoni can highlight a possible link between a symptom you have logged and a medication on the list — for example, flagging that dizziness is a known effect of a drug being taken. This comparison runs against a fixed reference list, on your own device, and nothing about it is sent anywhere for analysis.

It is a prompt to ask a question, not a diagnosis, and it never makes a decision about you or changes anything in your records by itself. Always check with a doctor or pharmacist.

8. On your device

So the app works without a signal, Halmoni keeps a copy of your family’s records on your phone and syncs when you reconnect. That copy is protected by your device’s own encryption and passcode, and by the app lock if you enable it. It is wiped when you delete your account or sign out.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account:

  • If other members remain in your family, your account and membership are removed. The records your family has already logged stay with them, because they are the family’s shared history and not yours alone.
  • If you were the last member, the entire family is deleted — every parent profile, medication, dose, appointment, symptom and note.
  • The copy on your device is wiped either way.

Deleted data may persist in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days before those backups expire, after which it is gone. Waiting-list emails are deleted once we have launched and told you, or sooner if you ask.

10. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, delete or restrict your information, object to how we use it, and ask for it in a portable form. Most of this you can do directly in the app. For anything else, write to privacy@halmoni.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to your data protection regulator. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is enforced per row in the database. Sign-in uses one-time email codes, so there is no password to steal. You can add a Face ID / Touch ID lock to the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

12. Children

Halmoni is for adults coordinating care. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. The person being cared for may be any age; the account holder must be an adult.

13. Changes

If we change this policy materially we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
